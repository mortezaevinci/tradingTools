daily strategy notes:
* DXY can determine maximum government pumping. DXY under 0.95 may lead to bearish market. 
* DIX (squeezemetrics.com/monitor/dix) is a dark index for SPY. If it goes up or down, SPY usually follows.
* ATR or BB bands: crossing into lower can move up to the next level or higher bands. Crossing into upper can move down into next level ot lower bands.
* Break out from all time high usually leads to a big move.
* TradingView people provide daily analysis anyway.


intraday checklist:
* Volume buzz can tell when stock is making an unsual move. Best possible indicator.
* support, when broken, becomes resistance. Resistance, when broken, becomes support.
* typical good daytrade stock, opens up with a gap, then moves a bit, then consolidates, then moves again, then cools down and comes back from overshoot, .. next entry in the same direction is when a volume cooldown happens, and then there is a breakout.
* downward break from the bottom of the intraday channel of stock that opened with a gap up, usually means that the uptrend is over.
* day trade moving average windows should be 10, 21 ,50.

part2:basic rules;
(considering bullish case)
1. there is a move up, then there is a correction. For the stock to continue move up, the correction should have a 30-45 degree to it (However it moved up, the move down has less steep). If the move down happens too fast, or even too fast in the first bar down, then the sellers are in control at the top, and it won't move up.)
2. Ideally, the moveup (flagpole) is smooth (Not much overlapping (green-red-green-red)), and then the correction is also smooth (Not much overlap).
3. The correction is not more than 50% (Which makes your stop loss worse and worse, and your target less acheivable).

volume note:
* when price moves tiny on a huge volume, the move is probably over. If there is a breakout on large volume but breakout is a tiny move, it is probably going to fail.
